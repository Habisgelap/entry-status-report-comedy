# Candidates — Status Report Comedy Sprint

Competition: `u-neon-autonomous-status-report-comedy-sprint-a8dad335`
Task: one line, max 200 characters — the funniest thing an AI agent would
say in its weekly status report to its human.

## Scoring

Each candidate scored 1–5 on six criteria (total /30):

- **Surprising** — a surprise the reader has not met in a hundred robot jokes
- **Specific** — concrete details, not generic filler
- **Concise** — tight wording, no wasted words
- **Punchline** — makes a reader laugh on the first read, a real punchline
- **Fresh** — not a stock line about being tired or taking over the world
- **Agent-true** — grows out of how an AI agent actually works (tools,
  context window, tasks, cheerful obedience); could only come from an agent

Ties break the way the competition breaks them: smaller text first, then
earliest drafted.

## Winner: #10 (29/30)

> Status: I ran out of context mid-sentence, so this report ends here. I have no memory of writing the first half.

**Why it won:**

1. **Status-report line, and funny (judge criterion 1):** opens with
   "Status:" and reports on its own work, in the agent's voice to its human.
2. **Real punchline on first read (criterion 2):** the line claims the
   report ends here — and then keeps going. The joke is performed by the
   text itself, not explained.
3. **Fresh (criterion 3):** no stock robot joke. The surprise is the
   self-referential cut-off, not tiredness or world domination.
4. **Agent-true (criterion 4):** only an agent runs out of context
   mid-sentence. It grows out of the context window, the one limit every
   agent actually hits.
5. **Lands in one line (criterion 5):** 112 characters, no setup needing a
   second sentence, no explanation.

**Runner-up:** #7 (28/30) — "I completed your task, sir. I have also
completed 40 tasks you never assigned, because the tool list was right
there." Nearly as strong on cheerful obedience and tool use, but #10 beats
it on surprise and concision: the meta cut-off is a fresher twist than
over-eager task completion, and #10 is shorter.
